distributed under MIT. Source: https://github.com/RelativityMC/raknetify at
`76ab30a` and its pinned netty-raknet submodule, plus the Blockfield patches;
release: https://github.com/Blockfield/raknetify/releases/tag/bf3.
Retain the bundled license and dependency notices when redistributing its JAR
or a bundle containing it. (Only the Velocity plugin is GPLv3; it is not used here.)
